## 🤔 Understanding the Name

### Origin & Meaning

**StageEgo** is a compound term that captures the essence of performance coaching:

#### Stage = Platform + Moment + Spotlight
- Your literal or metaphorical stage (theater, podium, screen, room)
- The moment when you need to perform at your highest level
- Any situation where your presence matters

#### Ego = Self + Presence + Consciousness
- Your psychological "self" (Latin: ego = "I am")
- Your authentic presence and identity
- Your inner voice and confidence
- Your conscious awareness of how you show up

#### Together = You at Your Best
**StageEgo represents you performing at your highest potential—confident, authentic, impactful.**

## 📝 Voice & Tone

### How StageEgo Communicates

#### Tone: Professional Yet Supportive
- **Confident**: We know coaching works
- **Honest**: We give real feedback, not just praise
- **Encouraging**: We believe in your potential
- **Clear**: We explain clearly without jargon
- **Authentic**: We're real, not corporate-speak

#### Voice Examples

**❌ Don't Say:**
- "Excellent performance, you're doing great!" (too generic)
- "Your pacing was suboptimal" (too technical)
- "Maybe try different vocal inflections?" (too vague)

**✅ Do Say:**
- "Your pacing improved in the second half. Try applying that energy from the start." (specific)
- "Slow down your speech rate by 15%. You're losing your audience during complex points." (actionable)
- "That pause worked. Lean into silence—it builds anticipation." (validating + coaching)

#### Copy Principles
1. **Specificity** — Give exact, actionable feedback
2. **Honesty** — Say what needs to improve
3. **Encouragement** — Acknowledge effort and progress
4. **Clarity** — Explain the "why" behind suggestions
5. **Respect** — Treat the performer with dignity

## 🔤 Naming Conventions

### Feature Naming
- Use "coach" terminology, not "chat" or "assistant"
  - ✅ "Choose Your Coach"
  - ✅ "Coaching Mode"
  - ✅ "Performance Feedback"
  - ❌ "Chat Mode", ❌ "Personality Select"

- Use "session" not "chat"
  - ✅ "New Session"
  - ✅ "Session History"
  - ❌ "New Chat", ❌ "Conversations"

- Use "performance" and "presence" terminology
  - ✅ "Your Presence"
  - ✅ "Stage Presence"
  - ✅ "Performance Coach"
  - ❌ "AI Chat", ❌ "Assistant Mode"

### Product Naming
- **Official**: StageEgo (capital S, capital E, one word)
- **Casual**: "the app", "the coach", "StageEgo"
- **Never**: "agent-claww", "Agent Claww", "AI Chat App"

## 🖼 Logo & Branding

### Primary Logo
- **Symbol**: 🎭 (Theatre masks)
- **Color**: Teal gradient or solid teal
- **Usage**: App icon, header, brand marks
- **Alternatives**: 
  - Stylized masks
  - Spotlight with figure silhouette
  - Stage with confident figure

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo**: Icon + "StageEgo" text (horizontal or stacked)
2. **Icon Only**: 🎭 for favicons, app launchers
3. **Text Only**: "StageEgo" in bold sans-serif (fallback)

### Do's & Don'ts
- ✅ Use teal (#14b8a6) or cyan (#06b6d4) for the mark
- ✅ Maintain clear spacing around the logo
- ✅ Use full logo for headers, icon for UI
- ❌ Never distort or rotate the logo
- ❌ Never change colors or proportions
- ❌ Never use against non-contrasting backgrounds

## 🎬 The StageEgo Experience

### Ideal User Journey
1. **Attraction**: User sees "StageEgo" and asks "What's that?"
2. **Discovery**: Learns it's an AI performance coach
3. **Interest**: Identifies with one coaching mode (presentation, acting, etc.)
4. **Trial**: Starts a session with a coach
5. **Engagement**: Gets honest, helpful feedback
6. **Habit**: Returns for multiple sessions
7. **Growth**: Sees measurable improvement in their performance
8. **Advocacy**: Recommends to others who perform

### Emotional Arc
- **Start**: Curiosity + some nervousness
- **Middle**: Focused engagement + honest feedback
- **End**: Empowered + confident + wanting more
